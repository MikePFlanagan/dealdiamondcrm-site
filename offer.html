Before you start, these answers

What happens right after I click "Start free"?

You email us and we reply with your login. Your account is created on the free Starter plan immediately — no credit card needed. If you decide Pro or Scale is right, you tell us and we switch you over when you're ready.

Is there a setup fee or a contract?

No setup fee, no contract. You're on free forever until you choose a paid plan, and paid plans are month-to-month unless you take the upfront discount.

How do discounts work if I pay up front?

You pay the discounted lump sum once and your plan stays active for that whole period. If you cancel early, unused time is credited to you.

What if I need to cancel?

One email, done. You keep access until the end of your paid period, and you can export all your data any time. Your deals belong to you.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. If you upgrade, you're billed the difference; if you downgrade, you keep the paid features until the period ends.

Who can I ask if I get stuck?

Community support is free on every plan. Pro and Scale get priority email support at hello@cuvane.com — real answers from real people.
