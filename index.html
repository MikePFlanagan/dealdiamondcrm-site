Questions investors ask

What is creative finance, and does DealFire support it?

Creative finance is buying real estate without a traditional bank loan — subject-to, seller financing, lease options, and partnerships. DealFire has a dedicated terms tracker for subject-to balances, seller-finance notes, payments, and exit triggers, so every deal's structure is on the record.

How do bird-doggers submit deals to me?

You give each bird-dogger a simple submit link. Their deal lands in your pipeline as a lead with them attached, so you always know what they brought in and what you owe them on close.

Can I migrate from my spreadsheet?

Yes. Import your leads and deals with a CSV upload — contacts, properties, values, and stages come across in one pass.

Is there a setup fee or long-term contract?

No setup fee and no lock-in. You pay monthly, or take a discount for paying up front. Cancel anytime.

Will this work for non-real-estate businesses?

DealFire is built for real estate first, and designed to extend to any pipeline-driven business — agencies, services, B2B sales — when you're ready to grow.
